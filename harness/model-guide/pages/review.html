Track A

Seeded-defect review results

A route reviews seeded diffs. The rank uses its worst pass. A route with fewer than three complete passes shows incomplete and takes no rank. A provisional pass keeps its rank.

The routes

Worst, median, and best are pass scores: the found plant weight over the set weight, less two points for each false claim. Blocker recall is the share of the blockers that the route found in its worst pass. False claims and real defects are sums over the passes.
